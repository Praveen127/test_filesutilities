About Compress PDF

Shrink PDFs that are too big to email or upload. Light compression removes unused data and keeps the text selectable; strong compression turns pages into optimized images, which works best for scans and photos. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop one or more PDFs into the compressor.
  2. Choose Light (keep text) or Strong, and a quality level for strong mode.
  3. Click Compress and download — the new size is shown for each file.

Frequently asked questions

Which mode should I choose?

Use Light for typed documents and Strong for scanned pages or PDFs full of photos.

What if my PDF doesn't get smaller?

If a file is already optimized, Fileutilo keeps your original rather than giving you a bigger file.
