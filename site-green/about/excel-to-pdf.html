About Excel to PDF

Share a spreadsheet as a PDF that anyone can open and nobody can accidentally edit. Each sheet becomes a table in the PDF. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your .xlsx, .xls, .ods or .csv file into the converter — PDF is already selected.
  2. Each sheet is laid out as a table.
  3. Download the PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Are all sheets included?

Yes. Every sheet in the workbook becomes a section of the PDF.

Are formulas kept?

The PDF shows the values the formulas produced, as a printed copy would.
