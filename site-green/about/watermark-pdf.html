About Watermark & numbers

Mark documents as CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT or COPY with a diagonal, horizontal or tiled watermark, and add page numbers such as "Page 3 of 9". Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop one or more PDFs into the tool.
  2. Turn on the watermark, page numbers or both, and set the text, size, colour and position.
  3. Click Apply and download the stamped PDFs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I skip numbering the cover page?

Yes. Tick "Don't number the first page" and choose the starting number.

Can I batch-watermark several PDFs?

Yes. Add several files and the same watermark and numbering are applied to each.
