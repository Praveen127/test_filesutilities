About PowerPoint to PDF

Send slides as a PDF that opens on any phone or laptop without PowerPoint. Each slide becomes a 16:9 page. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your .pptx (or .ppt) file into the converter — PDF is already selected.
  2. Each slide is turned into a page.
  3. Download the PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Are animations kept?

No — a PDF is a static document. Slide titles, text and images are laid out on 16:9 pages; animations and transitions are dropped, and complex slide designs are simplified.

Can I convert old .ppt files?

Yes, .ppt can be read and converted to PDF.
