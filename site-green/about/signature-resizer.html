About Resize your signature for an exam form

Exam and job forms want your signature at an exact size — often 140 × 60 pixels and 10–20 KB. Sign on white paper, take a photo, crop it, and Fileutilo makes a clean JPG that fits, with the paper turned white and the ink darker. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Sign on plain white paper with a black pen and take a clear photo.
  2. Drop the photo — the bank-exam signature size is already chosen; pick another exam if you need to.
  3. Crop around your signature, click Make my file and download.

Frequently asked questions

My form asks for 3.5 × 1.5 cm — which option is that?

Choose JEE Main or NEET signature; or pick Custom size and type the pixels your form lists.

Why does my signature look grey?

Keep Clean up the paper ticked — it turns the background white and the ink darker, which portals read more reliably.
