About Create ZIP

Pack many files, or an entire folder with its subfolders, into a single .zip that's easy to email or upload. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop files or a folder into the tool (or use Add a folder).
  2. Choose the compression level.
  3. Click Create ZIP and download it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I zip a whole folder?

Yes. Drop the folder and its subfolder structure is kept inside the ZIP.

Does zipping make files smaller?

Text and documents shrink a lot; photos and videos are already compressed, so they shrink little.
