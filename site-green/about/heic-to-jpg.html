About HEIC to JPG

iPhones save photos as HEIC, which many websites, forms and older computers can't open. Convert them to JPG in a click. Your photos are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

How it works

  1. Drop your .heic or .heif photos into the converter — JPG is already selected.
  2. Adjust the quality if you want smaller files.
  3. Click Convert and download the JPGs (several come as a ZIP).

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I open HEIC files on Windows?

HEIC is Apple's photo format and needs an extra codec on Windows. JPG opens everywhere.

Is the photo quality reduced?

At the default quality of 90 the difference isn't visible; lower it for smaller files.
