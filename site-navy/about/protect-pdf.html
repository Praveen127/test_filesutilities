About Protect PDF

Lock a PDF with a password before sharing it, using AES-256 encryption, and choose whether people may print, copy or edit it. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server. Your password is never sent anywhere either.

How it works

  1. Drop the PDF into the protect tool.
  2. Type a password and choose the permissions.
  3. Click Protect and download the encrypted PDF.

Frequently asked questions

How strong is the protection?

Fileutilo uses AES-256, the strongest encryption the PDF standard supports.

Can I remove the password later?

Yes, with the Unlock PDF tool, as long as you know the password.
