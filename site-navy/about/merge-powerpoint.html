About Merge PowerPoint

Bring together decks from different people or teams into one presentation. Drag the files into order and Fileutilo copies every slide — with its pictures, layouts, theme and animations — into a single .pptx. Keep each presentation’s own design, or give every slide the first deck’s design for a consistent look. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop two or more .pptx files.
  2. Drag them, or use the arrows, into the order you want, and choose how to handle the design.
  3. Name the file, click Merge presentations, and download it.

Frequently asked questions

Do the slides keep their design?

By default, yes — each presentation brings its own slide master and theme. Choose “Use the first presentation’s design” to put every slide on the first deck’s matching layouts instead.

Are speaker notes kept?

Notes of the first presentation are kept. Notes and comments of the second and later presentations are not carried over.

What if the presentations have different slide sizes?

The merged deck uses the first presentation’s size. Slides from a deck with a different shape keep their positions, so check those slides and adjust them if needed.
