About PDF to PowerPoint

Need to present a PDF, or reuse its pages in a deck? Fileutilo turns every page into a slide. Choose Same look to keep the page exactly as it is — fonts, charts and photos included — with the page’s text copied into the speaker notes, or Editable text to get headings, bullet points and tables you can change in PowerPoint. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop one or more PDFs.
  2. Choose Same look (each page becomes a slide picture) or Editable text.
  3. Click Convert to PowerPoint and download the .pptx — it opens in PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote and LibreOffice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit the text after converting?

With Editable text, yes — headings, paragraphs, lists and tables become real PowerPoint text. With Same look, each page is a picture so it looks identical; its text is in the speaker notes so you can copy it.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Same look works with any PDF, including scans. Editable text needs a PDF with real text — run OCR first for a scan.

What slide size will I get?

The slides take the shape of the PDF’s first page, so A4 portrait documents become portrait slides and wide reports become wide slides.
