About PNG to JPG

PNG screenshots and graphics can be large, and some forms only accept JPG. Convert them to JPG for a much smaller file that opens everywhere; transparent areas get the background colour you pick. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your PNG images — JPG is already selected.
  2. Pick the quality and a background colour for any transparent areas.
  3. Click Convert and download the JPGs, or all of them as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the JPG smaller?

JPG is designed for photos and compresses them much more than PNG. For screenshots with lots of text, keep quality high (90+) so the text stays crisp.

What happens to transparency?

JPG can’t be transparent, so those areas are filled with the background colour you choose — white by default.
