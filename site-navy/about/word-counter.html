About Word count

Get the word count, character count, sentences, reading time and most frequent words of a document or pasted text, in English, Kannada and other scripts. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Paste text, or drop a .docx, .pdf, .pptx, .odt, .rtf or .txt file.
  2. See the counts update instantly.
  3. Copy the statistics if you need them for a submission.

Supported formats and limits

Opens
DOCX, PDF, PPTX, ODT, RTF, TXT, pasted text
Creates
On-screen statistics
  • Scanned PDFs have no text to count — run OCR first.
  • Free: this tool has no daily limit.
  • Files are processed on your device, so very large files are limited by your browser and device memory.

Frequently asked questions

Does it count words in Kannada and Hindi?

Yes. Words are counted correctly in Indian scripts as well as Latin text.

Can it count words in a PDF?

Yes, for PDFs with real text. For scanned PDFs, run OCR first.

Is pasted text saved anywhere?

No. It stays in this browser tab and is gone when you close it; nothing is sent to a server.
