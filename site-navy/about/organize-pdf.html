About Organize pages

See every page as a thumbnail, drag pages into a new order, turn sideways scans upright and remove blank or unwanted pages. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop a PDF into the organizer to see its pages.
  2. Drag thumbnails to reorder; use the buttons to rotate or delete pages.
  3. Save and download the reorganized PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I fix a scanned page that is sideways?

Yes. Rotate any page left or right (or all pages at once) and the change is saved in the new PDF.

Are links and text kept?

Yes. Pages are moved, not re-drawn, so their text stays selectable.
