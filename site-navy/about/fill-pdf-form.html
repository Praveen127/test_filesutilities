About Fill in a PDF form

Application forms, bank forms and declarations often come as PDFs. Fileutilo lists a form’s fillable fields beside the page so you can type your answers; if the PDF isn’t fillable, click on the page to type, tick and date it instead. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop the PDF form.
  2. Type your answers into the fields on the right — or click Add text and click on the page for forms without fields.
  3. Optionally tick Flatten so the answers can’t be changed, then Save PDF.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my PDF is fillable?

When you open it, Fileutilo lists its form fields. If it says the PDF has no fillable fields, use Add text, Tick and Date on the page.

Can I sign the form too?

Yes — save it, then open it in Sign PDF to add your signature.
