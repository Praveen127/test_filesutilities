About PDF to Word

Get the text of a PDF back into Word so you can edit it — headings, paragraphs, lists and tables become real Word structure. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your PDF into the converter — DOCX is already selected.
  2. The text, headings, lists and tables are rebuilt as a Word document.
  3. Download the .docx and open it in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice.

Frequently asked questions

Are images kept?

No — PDF to Word keeps the text structure (headings, paragraphs, lists and tables) but not the images.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

A scanned PDF is a picture with no text. Run it through OCR first, then convert the text.
