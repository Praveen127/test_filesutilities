About JSONPath & schema

Test JSONPath expressions such as $.items[*].price against your data, or generate a JSON Schema from a sample document to validate future data. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Paste or open your JSON.
  2. Type a JSONPath expression, or switch to schema generation.
  3. Copy the matches or the generated schema.

Frequently asked questions

What is JSONPath?

A query language for JSON, like XPath for XML: $.store.book[0].title selects the first book's title.

What does the generated schema include?

Types, properties and array items inferred from your example, ready to refine and use for validation.

Can I use it to check JSONPath for my code?

Yes. Each match is listed with its full path (for example $.order.items[0].price), so you can confirm an expression before using it in a test or script.
