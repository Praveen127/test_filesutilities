About Remove metadata

Documents and photos carry hidden details: author and company names, revision history, camera model and the exact GPS location where a photo was taken. Remove them before you share. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop documents, PDFs or photos into the tool.
  2. Click Remove metadata.
  3. Check the report of what was removed and download the clean files.

Frequently asked questions

Which files are supported?

Word, Excel and PowerPoint (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx), OpenDocument files, PDF, JPG, PNG and WEBP.

Does it remove GPS location from photos?

Yes. EXIF data including GPS coordinates and camera details is removed.
