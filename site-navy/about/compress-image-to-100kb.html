About Compress an image to 100 KB

Many forms and websites cap uploads at 100 KB. Drop your picture and Fileutilo finds the highest quality that still fits under 100 KB, making the picture smaller only if it has to. You can also type exact pixels if the form asks for them. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your photo, signature or scan — the 100 KB limit is already set.
  2. If the form gives exact pixels, choose Width, Height or Fit and type them.
  3. Click Resize and download the image — the new size in pixels and KB is shown.

Frequently asked questions

Will the photo still look good at 100 KB?

Yes — Fileutilo searches for the highest quality that fits under 100 KB and makes the picture smaller only when quality alone isn’t enough. In the rare case it can’t fit, you get the smallest version and a message.

Can I do several photos at once?

Yes. Add several and each is compressed under the limit; download them together as a ZIP.

The form needs the photo between two sizes, like 20–50 KB — what should I use?

Use Passport size photo & signature: it makes the exact pixels and a size inside the allowed range for exam and job forms.
