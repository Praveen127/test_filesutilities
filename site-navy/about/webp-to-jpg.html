About WEBP to JPG

Images saved from websites are often WEBP, which some apps and upload forms reject. Convert them to JPG — or PNG if you need transparency. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your .webp images into the converter — JPG is already selected.
  2. Pick a background colour for transparent areas, or choose PNG to keep transparency.
  3. Click Convert and download the images.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to transparent areas?

JPG has no transparency, so transparent areas are filled with the background colour you choose (white by default). Choose PNG to keep them.

Can I convert many images at once?

Yes. Add several and download them together as a ZIP.
