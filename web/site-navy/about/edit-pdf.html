About Edit & fill PDF

Fill in a PDF form without printing it. If the PDF has fillable fields, they are listed beside the page so you can type your answers, tick boxes and choose options. For any other PDF, click on the page to add text, a tick or cross, or today’s date, and use white-out to cover a mistake. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop the PDF you want to fill in.
  2. Type into the form fields on the right, or click Add text, Tick or Date and click on the page.
  3. Drag items into place and click Save PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the existing text in a PDF?

Not directly — PDFs don’t store text like a Word file. Cover the old words with white-out and type the new text on top. To remove private text for good, use Mask PDF instead.

Can I type in Hindi, Kannada or other Indian scripts?

Yes, with Add text on the page. Standard PDF form fields can only show Latin letters here, so for other scripts leave the field empty and type on the page instead.

What does Flatten do?

It turns your answers into part of the page, so they can’t be changed in a PDF reader. Leave it off if someone else still needs to edit the form.
