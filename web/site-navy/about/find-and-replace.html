About Find & replace

Update a name, date or price in dozens of Word documents at once, or run a regular-expression replace over text and code files, then download the updated copies. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your .docx or text files into the tool.
  2. Type what to find and what to replace it with; set match case, whole words or regex.
  3. Count matches first if you like, then click Replace & download.

Frequently asked questions

Does it keep Word formatting?

Yes. Only the matching text changes; fonts, styles and layout stay the same.

Can I use regular expressions?

Yes. Tick Regular expression and use $1, $2 in the replacement for captured groups.
