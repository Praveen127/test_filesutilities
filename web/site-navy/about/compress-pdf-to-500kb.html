About Compress a PDF to 500 KB

Upload forms, job portals and banks often accept PDFs only up to 500 KB. Fileutilo first tries lossless clean-up that keeps the text selectable; if that isn’t enough, it turns pages into images at the highest quality that still fits under 500 KB. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your PDF — “Under a size limit” is already chosen with 500 KB.
  2. Change the limit if your form asks for something else.
  3. Click Compress and download the PDF that fits.

Frequently asked questions

Will the text still be selectable?

If clean-up alone gets the file under the limit, yes. Otherwise pages become images, which is what makes big scans small enough — the text then can’t be selected.

What if it can’t get under 500 KB?

You get the smallest version Fileutilo could make and a message. For long scans, split the PDF with Split PDF and compress each part, or upload fewer pages.
