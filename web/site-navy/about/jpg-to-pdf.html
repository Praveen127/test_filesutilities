About Images to PDF

Put phone photos, scans and screenshots into one tidy PDF — ideal for sending ID proofs, certificates or receipts. iPhone HEIC photos are supported. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your images into the tool.
  2. Drag them into order and choose the page size (A4, Letter or same as the image), orientation and margins.
  3. Click Create PDF and download it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert iPhone HEIC photos?

Yes. HEIC and HEIF photos open directly, alongside JPG, PNG, WEBP and other formats.

How do I make the PDF smaller?

Shrink the photos first with Resize & compress images, or run the finished PDF through Compress PDF.
