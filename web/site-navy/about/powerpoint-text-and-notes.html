About Slide text & speaker notes

Turn a presentation into a script, handout or summary. Fileutilo reads every slide in order — titles, bullet points, text boxes and tables — and, if you like, the speaker notes, and gives you a tidy .txt or Markdown file you can paste anywhere or feed into another tool. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop one or more .pptx files (old .ppt files work too, without notes).
  2. Choose plain text or Markdown, and whether to include speaker notes or skip hidden slides.
  3. Click Get text, then copy it or download the file.

Frequently asked questions

Does it include speaker notes?

Yes, when “Include speaker notes” is ticked — each slide’s notes follow its text.

What about text in pictures?

Text that is part of a picture can’t be read this way. Use PowerPoint to JPG and then OCR on those slides.

Why choose Markdown?

Markdown keeps the structure — slide headings, bullet levels and tables — and pastes neatly into Notion, GitHub, ChatGPT and many editors.
