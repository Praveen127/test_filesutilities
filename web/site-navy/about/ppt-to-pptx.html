About PPT to PPTX

Old .ppt presentations from PowerPoint 97–2003 won’t open in some apps and phones. Fileutilo reads the slide titles, text and bullet points from the old file and builds a fresh .pptx with one slide per slide, ready to edit in PowerPoint, Google Slides or Keynote. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your .ppt files into the converter — PPTX is already selected.
  2. Each presentation is read and rebuilt in your browser.
  3. Download the new .pptx files.

Frequently asked questions

Is the layout kept exactly?

No — the slide text and titles are carried over onto clean slides, but pictures and the original design are not. For an exact copy, open the .ppt in PowerPoint or LibreOffice Impress and use Save As → .pptx.

Can I convert a .ppt straight to PDF?

Yes — choose PDF instead of PPTX in the converter to get a document of the slide text.
