About JPG to PNG

Some apps, editors and upload forms want PNG. Convert JPG and JPEG photos to PNG without any further quality loss — handy before editing, or when a site insists on PNG. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your JPG images — PNG is already selected.
  2. Add as many as you like.
  3. Click Convert and download the PNGs, or all of them as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Will converting make my photo better?

No — PNG keeps exactly what is in the JPG without adding more compression, but it can’t bring back detail the JPG already lost. Expect a bigger file.

Can I make the background transparent?

Converting doesn’t remove backgrounds; the PNG looks the same as the JPG.
