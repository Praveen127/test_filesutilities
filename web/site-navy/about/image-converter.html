About Image converter

Change image formats in a click: iPhone HEIC to JPG, WEBP to PNG, PNG to ICO for favicons, or JPG to AVIF for smaller web images. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop one or more images into the converter.
  2. Choose the output format and quality.
  3. Click Convert and download the converted images.

Supported formats and limits

Opens
JPG, PNG, WEBP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF, GIF, BMP, TIFF, ICO, SVG
Creates
JPG, PNG, WEBP, AVIF, GIF, BMP, TIFF, ICO, SVG
  • WEBP and AVIF saving depend on your browser (Chrome and Edge support both).
  • SVG output wraps the picture in an SVG file; it doesn't trace it into vector shapes.
  • Free: 5 files of up to 20 MB each per task; 10 tasks a day without an account, 25 with a free account.
  • Pro: no Fileutilo limits on tasks, files or size. Files are processed on your device, so very large files are still limited by your browser and device memory.
