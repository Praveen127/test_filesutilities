About Merge Word

Combine chapters, reports or forms into a single .docx while keeping their formatting, images, tables and numbered lists. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your .docx files into the tool.
  2. Put them in order.
  3. Click Merge and download the combined Word document.

Frequently asked questions

Is formatting kept?

Yes. Styles, images, tables and lists from each document are carried into the merged file.

Can I merge old .doc files?

Convert them to .docx first with the Document Converter, then merge.
