About Compress a PDF to 200 KB

Government and exam portals often accept PDFs only up to 200 KB, 300 KB or 1 MB. Type the limit and Fileutilo first tries lossless clean-up that keeps the text; if that isn’t enough, it turns pages into images at the highest quality that still fits. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your PDF — “Under a size limit” is already chosen with 200 KB.
  2. Change the limit if your form asks for something else (100 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB…).
  3. Click Compress and download the PDF that fits.

Frequently asked questions

Will the text still be selectable?

If clean-up alone gets the file under the limit, yes. Otherwise pages become images, which is what makes big scans small enough — the text then can’t be selected.

What if it can’t get under the limit?

You get the smallest version Fileutilo could make and a message. For long scans, split the PDF into parts with Split PDF and compress each part.
