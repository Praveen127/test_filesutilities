About Sign PDF

Add your signature to a PDF without printing it. Draw it with a mouse or finger, type your name in a handwriting style, or upload a photo of your signature on paper — the background is removed for you. Then click where it goes, resize it, and add the date, your initials or any text. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop the PDF you need to sign.
  2. Click Add signature, then draw, type or upload it — tick Remember to keep it in this browser for next time.
  3. Click the page where it goes, drag to adjust, add the date or initials, then Save signed PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a legally valid signature?

It adds an image of your signature, like signing a printout and scanning it. That is accepted for many everyday documents. Where a law or organisation asks for a certificate-based digital signature (DSC) or Aadhaar eSign, you need that service instead.

Where is my signature saved?

Only if you tick Remember, and then only in this browser on this device. It is never sent to Fileutilo. You can forget a saved signature at any time.

Can I sign every page at once?

Yes. Place your initials or signature once, select it and click Copy to every page.
