About Checksums & Base64

Check that a download isn't corrupted or tampered with by comparing its checksum, or convert text and files to and from Base64. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop a file, or paste text.
  2. Read the MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512 and CRC-32 values, or paste a published hash to verify.
  3. Switch to Base64 to encode or decode.

Supported formats and limits

Frequently asked questions

How do I verify a download?

Drop the file and paste the published SHA-256 into Expected checksum; Fileutilo tells you if it matches.

Is it safe for private files?

Yes. Files are hashed in your browser and never uploaded.

Which checksum should I use?

Use SHA-256 to verify downloads and software. MD5 and SHA-1 are fine for spotting accidental changes, but are no longer considered secure.
