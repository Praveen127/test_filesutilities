About Word, PowerPoint & Excel converter

Turn a Word file into a PDF, a PDF into editable Word, a spreadsheet into CSV or a presentation into PDF — the input format is detected automatically. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop one or more files into the converter, or choose them from your device.
  2. Pick the format you need for each file, or apply one format to all.
  3. Click Convert, check the preview, and download the results.

Frequently asked questions

Which formats are supported?

Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, OpenDocument, RTF, HTML, plain text and PostScript. Scanned PDFs need OCR first.

Will my PDF have selectable text?

Yes. Word documents converted to PDF keep real, selectable text with headings, lists, tables and images rather than flat pictures.
