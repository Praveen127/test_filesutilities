About OCR

Read the text in scanned documents, phone photos and image-only PDFs, including Kannada, Hindi and other Indian languages, and save it as a text file you can edit and search. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server. The language data downloads once, the first time you use it.

How it works

  1. Drop scanned PDFs or photos into the OCR tool.
  2. Tick the languages used in the document.
  3. Click Read text, then copy the text or download it as .txt.

Supported formats and limits

  • Free: 5 files of up to 20 MB each per task; 10 tasks a day without an account, 25 with a free account.
  • Pro: no Fileutilo limits on tasks, files or size. Files are processed on your device, so very large files are still limited by your browser and device memory.

Frequently asked questions

Which languages are supported?

English plus Indian languages such as Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi and Bengali, among others.

Why is the first run slower?

Each language's data (2–15 MB) downloads the first time you use it; after that it's cached by your browser.
