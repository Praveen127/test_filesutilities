About Table cleaner

Tidy messy spreadsheets without formulas: remove duplicate and empty rows, trim stray spaces, fix capitalisation, find and replace, filter and sort — with undo. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Open a CSV or Excel file, or paste rows copied from a spreadsheet.
  2. Apply the clean-ups you need: trim, remove duplicates or empty rows, sort, filter, replace.
  3. Export the cleaned table as CSV, Excel or JSON.

Supported formats and limits

Opens
CSV, TSV, XLSX, XLS, pasted rows
Creates
CSV, XLSX, JSON
  • Formulas and formatting in Excel files are not kept; you get the values.
  • Free: this tool has no daily limit.
  • Files are processed on your device, so very large files are limited by your browser and device memory.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove duplicate rows?

Open the file and click Remove duplicate rows. Made a mistake? Use Undo.

Will it change my original file?

No. You download a new, cleaned copy; the original is untouched.

Can I paste rows straight from Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. Copy the cells and paste them in; columns are split on tabs, so the table keeps its shape.
