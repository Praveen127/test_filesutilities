About Exam photo & signature

Online application forms reject a photo or signature that’s the wrong size. Choose your exam and document, crop your picture, and Fileutilo makes a JPG with the exact pixels and a file size inside the allowed KB range — for example 200 × 230 px and 20–50 KB for a bank exam photo. It can also clean up the paper behind a signature and add your name and date below a photo. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Choose your exam and the document — photo, signature, thumb impression or declaration.
  2. Drop your picture and drag the frame to crop it.
  3. Click Make my file and download — the size check shows the pixels and KB.

Frequently asked questions

Which exams are included?

Bank exams (IBPS, SBI, RBI, RRB), SSC, UPSC, NEET UG and JEE Main, plus passport-size photos and a custom size. The sizes follow the 2025–26 notifications. Rules change, so always check your own notification.

My file is under the minimum KB — what happens?

Very small pictures can come out below the minimum. Fileutilo then adds empty padding to the file so it meets the minimum size; the picture itself doesn’t change.

Is it really free?

Yes. The exam photo and signature resizer has no daily limit, with or without an account.
