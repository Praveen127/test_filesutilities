About Merge PDF

Join two or more PDFs into a single document — handy for applications, reports and scanned pages. Drag the files into order and download one combined PDF. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your PDFs into the merge tool.
  2. Drag them, or use the arrows, to set the order.
  3. Name the file and click Merge PDFs, then download it.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

Free use allows 5 files of up to 20 MB each per task. Pro removes Fileutilo's caps; because merging happens on your device, its memory sets the practical limit for very large files.

Is merging PDFs here safe for confidential files?

Yes. The merge runs entirely in your browser, so the documents never reach our servers.
