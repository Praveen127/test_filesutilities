About Split PDF

Pull out just the pages you need, break a long PDF into one file per range, or cut it into equal chunks. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop the PDF into the split tool.
  2. Choose: extract pages into one PDF, one PDF per range, or every N pages.
  3. Type the page ranges (for example 1-3, 5, 8-) and click Split PDF.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write page ranges?

Use commas between ranges: 1-3, 5, 8- means pages 1 to 3, page 5, and page 8 to the end.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are, so text and images keep their original quality.
