About Extract images from PowerPoint

Need the photos, logos or diagrams someone put in a presentation? Fileutilo pulls out the original picture files stored inside the .pptx — exactly as they were inserted, without the quality loss of a screenshot — and gives them to you one by one or as a ZIP. It also works with Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) files, and can include videos and sounds. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop one or more .pptx files (or .docx and .xlsx).
  2. Optionally skip small icons, or leave out videos and sounds.
  3. Click Extract images and download them, or all of them as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Are the images the original quality?

Yes. They are the exact files stored inside the presentation, byte for byte — nothing is re-saved or resized.

Why do some pictures have an .emf or .wmf ending?

Those are Windows drawing formats that PowerPoint uses for some charts and clip art. They open in Office and Windows apps, but not in most web browsers.

Can I get the pictures from just one slide?

The tool takes every picture in the file. To get one slide as a picture instead, use PowerPoint to JPG with that slide number.
