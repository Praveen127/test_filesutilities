About Compress PowerPoint

Presentations get huge because of photos pasted at full camera resolution. Fileutilo resizes and re-saves those pictures at a sensible size for screens, turns large PNG photos into JPG, and packs the file tightly — usually making a deck many times smaller while it looks the same on a projector. Slides, text, animations, transitions and speaker notes are left exactly as they are. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop one or more .pptx files.
  2. Choose Light, Recommended or Strong compression.
  3. Click Compress — the before and after size is shown for each file — and download.

Frequently asked questions

Will my slides look different?

With Recommended, pictures are kept up to 1920 pixels wide, which is sharper than most projectors and screens can show. Use Light for printing or very large screens, and Strong when the file must be as small as possible.

Are animations and notes kept?

Yes. Only the pictures inside are optimised; slides, layouts, animations, transitions and speaker notes are untouched.

Why didn’t my file get smaller?

If its pictures are already small, or most of the size is video or embedded fonts, there is little to save. Fileutilo then gives you back your original file rather than a bigger one.
