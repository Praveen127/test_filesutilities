About PowerPoint to PDF

Send slides as a PDF that opens on any phone or laptop without PowerPoint. Fileutilo draws every slide — text, pictures, shapes, tables and backgrounds — onto its own PDF page in the slide’s shape, so the PDF looks like your presentation. Pick just some slides if you like. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your .pptx file — PDF is already selected.
  2. Optionally type which slides to include (for example 1-10) and the picture width.
  3. Click Convert slides and download the PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Will the PDF look like my slides?

Yes for text, pictures, shapes, tables and backgrounds. Charts, SmartArt effects and 3-D models may be simplified, and fonts you don’t have installed are replaced by similar ones.

Can I select the text in the PDF?

No — each slide is placed on its page as a picture so it looks right everywhere. If you need selectable text instead of the design, use the document converter with PDF chosen.

Can I convert old .ppt files?

Save them as .pptx in PowerPoint or LibreOffice first, or use the document converter, which reads the text of .ppt files.
