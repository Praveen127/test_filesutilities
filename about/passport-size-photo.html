About Passport size photo & signature

Online application forms reject a passport size photo or signature that’s the wrong size. Choose your exam and document, crop your picture, and Fileutilo makes a JPG with the exact pixels and a file size inside the allowed KB range — for example 200 × 230 px and 20–50 KB for a bank exam passport size photo. It can also clean up the paper behind a signature and add your name and date below the photo. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Choose your exam and the document — passport size photo, signature, thumb impression or declaration.
  2. Drop your picture and drag the frame to crop it.
  3. Click Make my file and download — the size check shows the pixels and KB.

Frequently asked questions

Which exams are included?

Bank exams (IBPS, SBI, RBI, RRB), SSC, UPSC, NEET UG and JEE Main, plus a standard 35 × 45 mm passport size photo and a custom size. The sizes follow the 2025–26 notifications. Rules change, so always check your own notification.

My file is under the minimum KB — what happens?

Very small pictures can come out below the minimum. Fileutilo then adds empty padding to the file so it meets the minimum size; the picture itself doesn’t change.

How many can I make for free?

Each file counts as one task, like the other tools: 10 a day without an account, 25 a day with a free account, and no daily limit on Pro.
