About PowerPoint to JPG

Share slides on WhatsApp, Instagram or a website, drop them into a document, or send a deck to someone without PowerPoint. Fileutilo draws each slide as a picture at the size you choose — 1280, 1920 or 3840 pixels wide — or puts them all into one PDF. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your .pptx file.
  2. Choose JPG, PNG or PDF, the width, and optionally which slides (for example 1-3, 5).
  3. Click Convert slides and download the pictures, one by one or as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Will the pictures look exactly like PowerPoint?

Text, pictures, shapes, tables and backgrounds are drawn faithfully. Charts, SmartArt effects and 3-D models may be simplified, and fonts you don’t have installed are replaced by similar ones — for pixel-perfect output of those slides, export them from PowerPoint itself.

Can I convert just a few slides?

Yes. Type the slide numbers or ranges, such as 2-4, 7. Leave the box empty for all slides.

Which format should I choose?

JPG gives smaller files for photos and sharing; PNG keeps text and lines perfectly sharp; PDF puts every slide into one document that’s easy to print or email.
