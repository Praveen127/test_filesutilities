About Open ZIP

See what's inside a ZIP without extracting everything: browse folders, preview text and images, and download only the files you need. Works with JAR, EPUB, DOCX and APK too. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop the .zip (or .jar, .epub, .docx, .apk) file into the tool.
  2. Browse the folders and preview files.
  3. Tick the files you want and download them.

Supported formats and limits

Opens
ZIP, JAR, EPUB, APK, DOCX/XLSX/PPTX
Creates
The files inside
  • Password-protected and RAR/7z archives are not supported.
  • Free: this tool has no daily limit.
  • Files are processed on your device, so very large files are limited by your browser and device memory.

Frequently asked questions

Can it open password-protected ZIPs?

Not at the moment; it opens standard, unencrypted ZIP archives.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser on phones and computers alike.

Can I look inside a Word file or an e-book?

Yes. DOCX, XLSX, PPTX and EPUB files are ZIP archives inside — open one to see and extract its images and parts.
