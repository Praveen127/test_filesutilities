About Compare files

Spot the differences between two versions of a contract, report, CSV or source file, side by side or unified, with options to ignore whitespace and case. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Open, drop or paste the original version on the left.
  2. Open, drop or paste the changed version on the right.
  3. Click Compare and step through the changes.

Supported formats and limits

Opens
TXT, CSV, JSON, code, DOCX, PDF, pasted text
Creates
Side-by-side or unified comparison
  • Word and PDF files are compared as text, so formatting-only changes don't show.
  • Free: this tool has no daily limit.
  • Files are processed on your device, so very large files are limited by your browser and device memory.

Frequently asked questions

Can it compare two Word or PDF files?

Yes. The text is extracted from .docx and .pdf files and compared line by line.

Can I ignore spacing changes?

Yes. Tick Ignore whitespace (and Ignore case if you like).

Is it safe for confidential contracts?

Yes. Both versions are compared inside your browser and neither file is uploaded.
