About Unlock PDF

Save an unlocked copy of a password-protected PDF that you have the right to open, without printing, copying or editing restrictions. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop the locked PDF into the unlock tool.
  2. Enter the PDF's password if it asks for one.
  3. Click Unlock and download the unprotected copy.

Frequently asked questions

Can it open a PDF without the password?

No. You need the password to open the file. PDFs protected with RC4, AES-128 and AES-256 are supported.

Is my password stored?

No. It's used inside your browser for that file only and never sent to a server.
