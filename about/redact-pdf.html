About Mask PDF & Aadhaar

Hide private details before you share a document. Drag boxes over anything, search for a name or number to mask it everywhere, or let Fileutilo find Aadhaar numbers and mask the first 8 digits — the masked format UIDAI recommends sharing. Masked pages are rebuilt as images (this is called redaction), so the hidden text is really gone, not just covered. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop a PDF, or a photo or scan of a document.
  2. Drag boxes over private details, search for words, or click Find Aadhaar numbers.
  3. Check every page, then click Save masked file.

Frequently asked questions

Is the hidden text really removed?

Yes. Each page with a box is rebuilt as an image with the box burned in, so the text underneath can’t be copied, searched or uncovered. Pages without boxes are kept as they are, and document details such as author and title are removed.

How does Aadhaar masking work?

Fileutilo looks for 12-digit numbers in the Aadhaar format, checks them with the Aadhaar check digit, and covers the first 8 digits so only the last 4 show. For scans and photos it reads the page with OCR first. Always check the result — OCR can miss blurry numbers.

What about the QR code on an Aadhaar card?

The QR code can contain your details. In browsers that can read QR codes, Fileutilo covers it for you; otherwise drag a box over it.
