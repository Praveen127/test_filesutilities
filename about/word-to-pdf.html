About Word to PDF

Turn a Word document into a PDF that looks the same on every device — ready to email, print or upload. The PDF keeps real, selectable text, headings, lists, tables and images. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your .docx (or .doc, .odt, .rtf) files into the converter — PDF is already selected.
  2. Wait a moment while each file is converted in your browser.
  3. Download each PDF, or all of them as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Will the PDF look exactly like Word?

Text, headings, lists, tables and images are kept. Complex layouts such as text boxes or multiple columns are simplified.

Can I convert several Word files at once?

Yes. Add several files and each becomes its own PDF; free use allows 5 files per task.
