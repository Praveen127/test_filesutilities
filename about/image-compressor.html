About Resize & compress

Online forms often need a photo under 50 KB or a signature under 20 KB. Set the dimensions and a maximum file size, and Fileutilo finds the best quality that fits. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Drop your photo or signature image into the tool.
  2. Pick a preset (Signature ≤ 20 KB, Photo ≤ 50 KB…) or set your own width, height and KB limit.
  3. Click Resize and download the image.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a photo under 50 KB?

Choose the "Photo ≤ 50 KB" preset, or type 50 in Max file size, and click Resize.

Which format should I use for forms?

JPG is accepted almost everywhere and gives the smallest files for photos.
