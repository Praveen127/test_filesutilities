      PRIVACY, PRECISELY

      What stays on your device — and what doesn't

      “Private” should be checkable. Here is exactly what Fileutilo sends over the network, and what it never does.

      Stays on your device

      • Your files — PDFs, documents, images, spreadsheets, archives
      • Their contents and file names
      • Passwords you type into Protect or Unlock PDF
      • The results — saved straight from the page to your downloads
      • Your favourites and recent activity — kept in this browser only, and you can clear them

      This is true for all 37 tools, on every plan, signed in or not.

      GOOD TO KNOW

      Questions people ask

      Do I need to install anything?

      No. Fileutilo runs in any modern browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone.

      Are my files uploaded?

      No. All 37 tools process files inside your browser. Fileutilo's servers only store account, usage-count and payment records if you sign in or buy something — never your files, their names or their contents.

      Why does a tool download something the first time?

      Some tools need processing code — for example the PDF engine or OCR language data. It's fetched from a public code library once and cached by your browser. Those downloads don't contain anything from your files.

      Can I use Fileutilo for free?

      Yes: 10 tasks a day without an account, 25 with a free account, and the data tools have no daily limit. Pro removes all limits and ads.

      Does it work offline?

      Once a tool's code has been loaded, it keeps working if your connection drops — another sign the work happens on your device.

      What's kept in each conversion?
      • To PDF — real, selectable text with headings, paragraphs, lists, tables and images (from Word). Presentations become wide 16:9 slide pages. Text in non-Latin scripts is rendered as page images so every character shows.
      • To HTML — from Word (.docx) the layout, tables and images are kept; other sources keep headings, paragraphs, lists and tables.
      • To DOCX, ODT, RTF, DOC — headings, paragraphs, lists, tables, line and page breaks. Images aren't carried over. DOC files are written in Rich Text format with a .doc name, which Word opens normally.
      • To XLSX / XLS — spreadsheets keep every sheet and its values; from documents, each table becomes a sheet and the remaining text goes to a "Text" sheet.
      • To PPTX — a title slide, then one slide per heading, page or slide; long text and big tables continue onto extra slides.
      • To TXT / PS — text only; tables become tab-separated (TXT) or column rows (PS, A4 pages).
      • From DOC, PPT and PS — text is extracted (best effort); legacy PPT can be read but not written — choose PPTX instead. Scanned PDFs contain no text and need OCR.

      Last updated: 25 November 2026

      Kalaburagi Labs (“we”) runs Fileutilo. This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

      Your files

      Files you open in Fileutilo are processed inside your web browser, on your own device. They are not uploaded to our servers and we cannot see them.

      What we collect when you create an account

      • Your email address, or phone number if you sign in with a one-time code, and your name if you give it or sign in with Google.
      • Your plan, credit balance, and a daily count of tasks per tool (not the files or their contents).
      • Payment records: product, amount, status, date and Razorpay reference numbers. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay; we never see or store them.

      Visit statistics (everyone)

      To understand how Fileutilo is used, we count page visits with Vercel Web Analytics: which page was viewed, the site you came from, and your country, browser and device type. It uses no cookies. Visits are told apart only by a code that changes every 24 hours, so you can’t be followed across days or other websites. It never receives your files or anything in them.

      We also use Google Analytics (Google LLC) to see which pages and tools are used and how many visits lead to sign-ups and purchases. It sets cookies and receives the page address, the tool name and plan or purchase amounts — never file names or file contents. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are measured only after agreeing in the consent message.

      Why we use it

      To provide your account, apply plan limits, process payments and refunds, prevent abuse, and meet legal and tax obligations.

      Who processes it for us

      • Vercel — hosting the website, and anonymous visit statistics (Vercel Web Analytics).
      • Supabase — sign-in and database hosting.
      • Razorpay — payments.
      • Google — “Sign in with Google”, Google AdSense ads shown on the free plan, and Google Analytics visit statistics where used.
      • Our SMS provider — sending one-time sign-in codes.
      • jsDelivr, unpkg and esm.sh — delivering the open-source code libraries the app uses.

      Cookies and storage

      Your sign-in session and tool settings are stored in your browser’s local storage. So are your favourite tools and your recent activity (the tool used, when, how many files, and the names of up to two result files) — they stay in that browser, are never sent to us, and can be cleared or turned off from the home page. If you install Fileutilo as an app, your browser also keeps a copy of the site and the tools’ code so they work offline. Vercel visit statistics don’t use cookies; Google Analytics, when used, does (see above). On the free plan, Google may use cookies to show and measure ads; see Google’s advertising policy for details. Pro and Lifetime users don’t see ads.

      How long we keep it

      Account data is kept until you delete your account. Payment records are kept for as long as tax laws require, without being linked to a deleted account.

      About Mask your Aadhaar number

      Hotels, landlords and offices often ask for a copy of your Aadhaar. A masked copy shows only the last 4 digits, which is the format UIDAI recommends sharing. Drop your Aadhaar PDF or a photo of the card, click Find Aadhaar numbers, and Fileutilo covers the first 8 digits — and the QR code where your browser can read it. Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

      How it works

      1. Drop your Aadhaar PDF, or a photo or scan of the card.
      2. Click Find Aadhaar numbers — the first 8 digits are covered on every page.
      3. Check the result, drag a box over anything else you want hidden, and save.

      Supported formats and limits

      Opens
      PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC
      Creates
      PDF or image
      • Masked pages become images, so their text is no longer selectable and the file can get bigger.
      • Automatic Aadhaar detection can miss blurry or handwritten numbers — always check every page.
      • Scanned pages and photos need OCR, which downloads about 4 MB the first time.
      • Free: 5 files of up to 20 MB each per task; 10 tasks a day without an account, 25 with a free account.
      • Pro: no Fileutilo limits on tasks, files or size. Files are processed on your device, so very large files are still limited by your browser and device memory.

      Frequently asked questions

      Is a masked Aadhaar valid as ID?

      UIDAI lets you download and share a masked Aadhaar that shows only the last 4 digits. Whether a particular office accepts it is up to them.

      Can someone undo the masking?

      No. The masked page is rebuilt as an image with the digits burned out, so they can’t be uncovered or copied.
